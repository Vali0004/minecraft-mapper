Search for something, or pick it from a food in the food planner.

Steps start from what the team has: everything in containers in its claimed chunks and in its members' inventories, ender chests and backpacks. The planner estimates material, power and step costs, then checks quantities and retries around shortages. It shows the best route it found, which may not be the cheapest possible route. Byproducts aren't counted. What's held is used first, what's short is made, and what's short and can't be made is planned around. It prefers a working Circuit Assembler to crafting circuits by hand. It uses the team's machines, and builds one only when there's no other way (power generation and a multiblock's structure aren't planned). Nothing gets taken apart, except emptying cells. Under "other ways", "plan with this" routes through that recipe instead. Water and GregTech programmed circuits (set in the machine) are free. Machines outside GregTech, the crafting grid and the furnace (Forestry, Thaumcraft…) aren't known.
