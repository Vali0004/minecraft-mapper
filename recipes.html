Steps start from what the team has: everything in containers in its claimed chunks and in its members' inventories, ender chests and backpacks. A step needs a machine the team owns, at the right voltage. The steps are the shortest chain, not the best yield; open "every recipe" and sort by yield for that. Water and GregTech programmed circuits (set in the machine) are free. Machines outside GregTech, the crafting grid and the furnace (Forestry, Thaumcraft…) aren't known.
